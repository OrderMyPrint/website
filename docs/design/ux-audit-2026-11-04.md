## 1. How the audit was done
1. **Automated scan of every screen on pages 04–11.** It checked for:
   - leftover DEMO, "Undecided", "set in admin", decision codes and [bracket] placeholders;
   - default component text;
   - text that overflows its screen;
   - collapsed (≈0 px) text boxes;
   - each screen's button labels.
2. **Number reconciliation.** Each configurator was checked in this order: tier table → price breakdown → GST → total. The same amounts were then traced through cart, checkout, confirmation, account, tracking and admin.
3. **Action inventory.** Every button or link that opens a decision, a form or a destructive action was checked. Each one must have a designed result: a dialog, sheet, drawer, banner or screen.
4. **Prototype rebuild.** The page 12 flows were rebuilt from the corrected screens. Each link was checked, and there are no dangling links (122 links in total).

## 2. Problems found and fixed

### 2.1 Flow and data consistency
| # | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Configurator tier tables, breakdowns and totals did not match. For example, Visiting Cards showed a breakdown of ₹1,400 + GST ₹252 but a total of ₹1,982.40. | Every product was recalculated from its tier price plus option prices. **Totals:** VC ₹1,652.00 · LP ₹3,540.00 · EN ₹1,829.00 · BR ₹11,210.00 · ST ₹1,298.00 · DTF ₹1,412.46 · TS ₹29,578.50. |
| 2 | All 7 mobile configurators showed the **Visiting Cards** tier table. | Each mobile configurator now shows its own product's tiers. The footnote names the product's unit ("per pad", "per sheet"…). |
| 3 | Cart lines included GST, but the summary added GST again. Delivery was left out of GST. Checkout steps 2–4 showed ₹3,759.28, while the cart showed ₹2,754.00. | Cart lines now **exclude GST**: VC ₹1,400 + Stickers ₹1,100 = ₹2,500. The summary is: PRINT10 −₹250 · delivery ₹99 · GST 18% on items + delivery = ₹422.82 · **total ₹2,771.82**. This total is used the same way in cart, checkout, payment, confirmation, account and admin. **The CA must confirm this treatment (D-28).** |
| 4 | Checkout **Delivery** step was missing its content. | Rebuilt the step with Standard (₹99) and Express (₹199) options, a "Delivering to … Edit" row, a delivery explainer and "Continue to review". The pickup option, which was undecided, was removed. |
| 5 | Quote Q-2026-0142 used different numbers on the customer and admin screens. | Both now use one set of numbers. **Lines:** T-shirts 250 × ₹275 (engine ₹289, −4.8%) and cards 2,000 × ₹3.99. **Charges:** screens ₹900, shipping ₹600. **Totals:** GST ₹5,026.90 · total ₹83,256.90. |
| 6 | Order amounts differed between account, orders list, admin orders, payments, invoices, refunds and audit log. | All of them now use the reconciled order totals. |
| 7 | Public tracking showed order 001234 "In production", but its OTP step led to the detail page for order 001190. | The tracking pages (desktop and mobile) now show OMP-2026-001190 with a timeline that matches its order detail. |
| 8 | The admin dashboard date said "Thursday 23 Oct 2026". | Corrected to **Friday**. |
| 9 | Customer self-cancel was only described in the policy. No screen offered it. | Added a "Cancel this item instead" action on the artwork-revision screen. The item can be cancelled before printing, with the refund amount shown. |

### 2.3 Content (continuation of the no-DEMO pass)
- **Pages cleaned:** Mobile, Tablet, Configurators, Cart & Checkout, Quotations, Account, Admin dashboard and Admin operations.
- **What was removed:** DEMO, "Undecided", "set in admin" and decision codes.
- **What replaced it:**
  - real option prices and turnaround ranges;
  - SEO-ready copy;
  - empty-state wording for admin settings that are not yet configured.
- **What remains on purpose:** legal facts that only the business can supply stay as visible placeholders. These are the support email, registered address, GSTIN and grievance officer (D-09). The map-tile area also stays a placeholder until D-27 is decided.

## 3. Dialogs, sheets, drawers and banners added (36)
